# Privacy Policy — Ginti

**Effective date:** 28 June 2026
**App:** Ginti (package `com.trackerexpo.app`)
**Developer contact:** edusystempro2@gmail.com

## Summary
Ginti is a **local-first, offline** expense tracker. All of your data stays **on your device**.
We do **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or financial information. There are no
accounts, no servers, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

## What data the app stores (on your device only)
The app stores the information you enter, in a private local database on your phone:
- Transactions you add (amount, note/description, category, payment source, who paid, date)
- Categories, payment sources, people, budgets and subscriptions you configure
- App settings (e.g. reminder preferences)

This data never leaves your device except when **you** explicitly choose to:
- **Export a backup or report** — you pick where it goes via the Android share sheet (e.g. Files,
  Drive, email). The app does not upload it anywhere on its own.
- **Import a backup** — you choose a file from your device storage.

## Permissions we use
- **Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS):** to show your daily spending recap and reminder nudges.
  These are scheduled locally on your device; no data is sent anywhere.
- **Vibrate:** subtle haptic feedback for notifications.

We do **not** request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, or background
location. The app does **not** read your notifications or messages.

## Data sharing
We do not share data with any third party. The app makes no network requests to our servers
(there are none).

## Data deletion
Because data is stored only on your device, you are in full control:
- Delete individual records inside the app, or
- Use **Settings → Clear All Data** to erase everything, or
- Uninstall the app to remove all associated data.

## Children's privacy
Ginti is a general-purpose finance utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not
knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new effective
date.
